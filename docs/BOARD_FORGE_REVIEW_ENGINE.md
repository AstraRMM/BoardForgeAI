# BoardForge Review Engine

BoardForge Engineering Review is a local artifact-based design critique. It grades schematic confidence, placement, routing, manufacturing, sourcing honesty, mechanical constraints, and documentation.

It is not certification and does not replace a licensed engineering or compliance review. Its job is to make local KiCad evidence understandable before a user decides to fabricate, revise, or publish a project.

Outputs:

- `BoardForge_Board_Review_Report.md`
- `BoardForge_Board_Review_Report.json`
